Questions about pricing

What does the pilot project cost?

Nothing. We build a pilot for one use case, free of charge and with no obligation. You test it with your own examples. Only if you say yes do we agree on a fixed price for the full solution.

What does unlimited revisions mean?

We revise designs, screens and features within the agreed scope until you approve them. Revision rounds are never billed extra.

Why do you show starting prices?

Every solution is built for your company. The final price depends on scope, such as integrations, user roles and languages. After the pilot you get a binding fixed price.

Do you charge per user?

No. Project prices are one-time and operations plans are per company. Your whole team can use the software at no extra cost. Only AI model usage depends on volume and is billed at cost.

Are prices including VAT?

No. All prices are in AED and exclude 5 % UAE VAT.

Do you offer Arabic?

Yes. Interfaces and AI assistants can be built in English and Arabic, including right-to-left layouts.
