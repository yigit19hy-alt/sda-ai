Site information that arrives too late

On a construction or fit-out project the information exists, but it is scattered: photos in group chats, snags on paper, timesheets in a notebook, permits in someone's inbox. By the time the office has a clear picture, the client is already asking why something is late.

We build mobile apps that site teams actually use: large buttons, voice notes, photos and offline mode. Everything is timestamped and GPS-tagged, and the office sees progress, snags and hours as they happen. AI turns voice notes into written reports and summarises the day for the project manager.

Common questions

Will site teams actually use it?

We design for the site: few fields, big buttons, photos and voice notes instead of typing, and Arabic, English or other languages your crews speak.

Does it work without internet on site?

Yes. The app works offline and syncs as soon as there is a connection.

Can it connect to our accounting or ERP system?

Yes. Approved timesheets and completed jobs can flow into your accounting or ERP system for invoicing and payroll.

Can subcontractors use it?

Yes, with their own logins and access limited to their projects and tasks.
