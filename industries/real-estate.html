Too many requests, too many channels

Real estate teams in the UAE handle a constant stream of enquiries from portals, WhatsApp and calls, often in several languages. Facility teams face the same on the operations side: tenant requests, maintenance tickets, contractor visits and inspections, tracked across spreadsheets and chat groups.

We build CRM and facility management software around how your team works, and add AI where it saves the most time: replying to new leads within seconds, qualifying them, booking viewings, triaging tenant requests and dispatching the right technician with the right information.

Common questions

Can the AI talk to leads on WhatsApp?

Yes. We connect to the official WhatsApp Business Platform. The assistant answers questions about a listing, qualifies the lead and books a viewing in your calendar.

Can it replace our current CRM?

It can, or it can work alongside it. Many teams start with AI lead handling connected to their existing CRM and replace other tools later.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes. Interfaces, messages and the AI assistant can work in Arabic and English, including right-to-left layouts.

How is tenant data protected?

Access is role-based, data is encrypted, and we host in the region you choose, in line with the UAE data protection law.
